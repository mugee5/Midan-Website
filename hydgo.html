A Midān Product · Hyderabad on the go

Your city,made legible.

HydGo answers the questions every Hyderabadi has and no app cleanly does: where am I, who governs here, and what's nearby. Open it anywhere in the city, no login and no clutter, and get verified civic information you can actually act on.

Request a demo See what it does ↓
Splash · Hyderabad on the go
01 — What It AnswersThree questions, one tap
Where am I?

Your exact civic identity the moment you open the app: mandal, revenue division, district, pincode, and GPS. The address behind the address.

Who governs here?

Your MLA and MP, and the offices actually responsible for your area — police station, GHMC circle, water board — each a tap away from a call.

What's nearby?

Services around you on a map bounded to your ward: police, hospitals, MeeSeva and Aadhaar centres, distances first, so you know what's actually reachable.

02 — Inside The AppBuilt for the person on the street

No accounts, no ads, no data games. Just the civic layer of Hyderabad, made quick to reach and honest by default.

Verified records

Accuracy over volume. Every office, boundary and contact is checked before it ships, because a wrong answer here costs someone a wasted afternoon.

Your ward on a map

See your mandal boundary drawn on the map, with every service pinned inside it, so "nearby" means nearby to you, not to the city centre.

One-tap leaders

Your elected representatives with a call or email button right there — no searching, no wondering who's actually responsible for your street.

Responsible services

Police, GHMC and the water board, each with what they handle and how far away they are, plus a way to report or ring them directly.

Emergency, one tap

Police, ambulance, fire, women's and child helplines, disaster management: the numbers that matter, ready to dial when seconds count.

Works on the ground

Built for real conditions: patchy connectivity, more than one language, on whatever phone you're holding. It meets the city where it is.
