02 — How It WorksThree steps, under a minute
01
Create a session

Open Interacto, build your polls, quizzes, and questions, and you instantly get a shareable session link and a QR code to put on screen.

02
Audience joins instantly

Participants scan the QR or open the link on any device — phone, laptop, tablet. No app to download, no account to create.

03
Respond, see results live

Answers appear on your dashboard in real time as bars fill and the leaderboard updates. Advance question by question, at your pace.

03 — FeaturesEverything in one link
Live polls

Multiple-choice polls with a real-time bar chart that fills as votes land. Perfect for reading the room in seconds.

Scored quizzes & leaderboard

Quizzes with correct answers, speed-based scoring, and a live leaderboard that turns any session into a little competition.

Open text & Q&A

Collect open-ended responses and questions from the floor — they stream into a feed, newest first, ready to read aloud.

QR join — no app

Put a QR on screen or drop the link in WhatsApp. Participants are in within seconds, on whatever device they're holding.

Real-time results

Every response updates the dashboard the instant it arrives — bars, counts, ratings, and Yes/No splits, all live.

Analytics & export

After the session, get a clean breakdown — participation rate, per-question results, the final leaderboard — and export it to CSV.
