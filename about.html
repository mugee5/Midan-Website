The Origin

Built from first principles.
Not a pivot.

Midān didn't start from a gap in the market. It started from a set of beliefs about what software should do and who it should serve. The products reflect that. So does the writing.

The thinking behind Midān spans community leadership, institutional design, policy interest, and a genuine preoccupation with why organisations fail the people they're supposed to serve. That's not a vertical — it's a way of looking at problems.

Midān is being built as a long-arc commitment, not a sprint. The plan has always been to build something that compounds over time and outlasts the moment it was started in.

What We Believe Five foundations
I — Value
Genuine value over manufactured attention

Every product Midān builds must create real benefit — not exploit time, not manufacture anxiety, not extract engagement. We don't compete for attention. We compete for trust. Those are different games with different winning conditions.

II — Integrity
Sufficiency and integrity as strategic assets

We don't need to manufacture dissatisfaction to sell our products. Operating from a position of principle — not urgency — is what allows us to hold the line on our findings and say no to the wrong partnerships. The long-term credibility that comes from that is worth more than whatever short-term growth we'd gain by abandoning it.

III — Sovereignty
Full creative and intellectual independence

Midān never compromises its findings, its data, or its positions for a funding partner, a government, or a client. We work with institutions — we are not owned by them. Independence is not a constraint on what we can build. It is the product.

IV — Design
Citizen-first by design, not by declaration

Whatever the paying client — a school, a corporation, a government department — the end beneficiary of every product is the person the system is supposed to serve. The parent. The student. The community. We build for them, even when they're not the ones writing the cheque.

V — Horizon
Legacy over transaction. The 20–30 year view.

Every decision is made with a 20–30 year horizon, not a quarterly one. The goal is to build something that outlasts the moment it was started in, that contributes to a more accountable set of institutions in the places it operates, and that represents a body of work someone can look back on with honest pride. We are not optimising for the next funding round.

How We Work

Built to last.
Independent by design.

Midān generates income through software — tools we own, build for multiple clients simultaneously, and improve over time. This is the financial engine that gives the company independence. Civic and institutional work happens on Midān's own terms, never as the primary revenue dependency.

The separation matters. A company that depends on a single large partner cannot say no to that partner. Midān's structure is designed to preserve the independence that makes the mission credible — so our findings and positions are always ours.

We are not a consultancy. We don't build custom things for individual clients and walk away. We build infrastructure — products that serve many simultaneously, that we own fully, and that compound in value over time. The boundary with institutional partners is always the same: open data, defined deliverables, and Midān retains analytical independence.
