Live · Participation
Interacto

Live participation for any room: classrooms, workshops, events.

Run polls, scored quizzes, and open Q&A your audience joins by scanning a QR code. No app, no account, no friction. Answers land on the presenter's screen in real time, a live leaderboard keeps the room with you, and every session exports clean afterwards. It's live, and it's free to try.

Civic Tech
HydGo

Your city, made legible, right in your pocket.

Open it anywhere in Hyderabad and it answers three questions: where am I, who governs this area, and what public services are nearby: government offices, hospitals, Meeseva and Aadhaar centres, emergency contacts. Accuracy over volume; every record verified before it ships, because a wrong answer here costs someone real time.
