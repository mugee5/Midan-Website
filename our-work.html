Our Work

Three domains.
One thread through all of them.

We work in education, healthcare, and the green economy. The connecting logic isn't the sector — it's the gap between what the institution claims and what the person it's supposed to serve actually receives. That gap is what we build into.

An honest note: Midān is early. The products below are in active development or planned — not live, not shipped. We're listing them here because we think the problems are real and worth naming in public, and because we'd rather be honest about where we are than pretend otherwise. When these ship, this page will say so.

01
Education

School operations are held together
by individual effort
that doesn't compound.

Schools — especially independent and growing institutions — run on a patchwork of informal systems, disconnected tools, and individual effort that doesn't compound. Communication with families breaks down. Administrative tasks are repeated manually. The people running these schools are capable; the infrastructure around them isn't.

The gap isn't a technology problem — it's an infrastructure one. The right tools, assembled thoughtfully and priced for the institutions that actually need them, can change what a school is operationally capable of.

In development
The Problem We're Solving
Operational infrastructure for schools

Tools that give school leaders a real operational backbone — from communicating with families, to managing enrolment, to producing personalised student records. Built for the schools that need it most: independent and growing institutions that are too complex for nothing and too lean for enterprise software.

What makes it different
Designed around how these schools actually operate, not how enterprise assumes they do
Priced and scoped for institutions without dedicated IT teams
AI-assisted where it saves real time — not as a feature, as a function
Built to be adopted by people running schools, not managing software
02
Green Economy

Sustainability reporting is
now mandatory.
The infrastructure isn't ready.

Regulatory pressure to report environmental, social, and governance data is real and growing. Companies are being asked to produce structured, auditable sustainability disclosures — but most are still trying to meet these requirements manually, without audit trails, and without any way to verify the data they're submitting.

This is a compliance problem with a regulatory forcing function. Demand doesn't require convincing anyone the problem exists. What's missing is the infrastructure to meet it honestly, consistently, and at scale.

In development
The Problem We're Solving
Sustainability & accountability reporting infrastructure

A platform for companies to track, structure, and report environmental and governance data for compliance — and beyond it. Structured data collection, built-in calculation methodology, audit trails throughout, and output formatted for regulatory submission. Built for organisations that need to get this right, not just done.

Why it fits Midān specifically
Making invisible things measurable and accountable — the same DNA as everything else we build
Regulatory pressure creates genuine demand without us having to evangelise the problem
Accountability is built into the structure, not added as a feature
Designed to be auditable from day one — not retrofitted later
03
Healthcare

Health data is collected.
The people it's about
never see it.

Community health programmes in India generate significant operational data — patient visits, treatment cycles, referral outcomes, programme coverage — but that data almost never flows back to the communities being served, or to the frontline workers making daily decisions. It flows upward to funders and administrators, stripped of the context that would make it useful.

The gap isn't between rich and poor data — it's between data that serves institutions and data that serves people. We're building toward infrastructure that reverses that direction: starting with what the frontline worker and the patient actually need to know.

Planned
The Problem We're Solving
Community health data infrastructure

Operational and outcomes tracking for community health programmes — built with frontline workers rather than around them. Structured data collection, accessible interfaces, programme dashboards that surface what's actually happening at the point of care, and accountability reporting that doesn't require a separate data team to produce.

What makes this different
Designed with the people doing the work — not imposed on them from above
Data flows to the patient and frontline worker, not only upward to administrators
Built for real-world conditions — low-connectivity, multilingual, high-pressure environments
Accountability built in — funders can verify programme claims, not just receive reports
