Who We Work With

Three kinds of partners.
One shared purpose.

Early Users
Schools, Facilitators & Civic Institutions

The institutions and people facing the problems we're building into. We want early users who'll tell us honestly when something isn't working, and be the first proof that it does. In exchange: early access, a real say in the roadmap, and pricing that reflects the relationship.

Domain Partners
NGOs, Researchers & Ground Truth Holders

Organisations, including government and public bodies, with deep roots in education, civic life, or AI governance. You bring the reach and field knowledge we can't fake; we bring the technical side. We love this kind of collaboration and take it on gladly.

We're not formally hiring yet, but we're always interested in people who want to build for the long run: developers, designers, researchers, and anyone who understands the Indian institutional context from the inside. If that's you, write and tell us what you'd want to build here.
